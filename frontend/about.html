Portfolio project

Explore India was built to make discovery feel personal.

A full-stack travel discovery and planning experience created by Kiran—combining visual storytelling with real accounts, saved places and itinerary planning.

Explore the product →

The idea

One place to move from inspiration to a plan.

India has extraordinary range. This project turns that range into a calm, photo-led catalogue, then gives visitors the tools to make their own shortlist and trip plans.

01

Explore

Searchable destinations, regional stories, culture, food and image-led detail pages.

02

Save

Accounts with persistent saved places and a profile that reflects each traveller’s collection.

03

Plan

Personal trip plans with travel month, budget, pace and a day-by-day itinerary outline.

04

Manage

An administration area for destination visibility and traveller enquiries.

What it demonstrates

Designed and built end to end.

  • Responsive interface design and accessible navigation
  • Node.js and Express API development
  • SQLite persistence, authenticated sessions and role-aware access
  • Thoughtful empty, error and success states
